## 1/4

♿ Amsterdam Metro Live just got friendlier for everyone! 💛

📸 Press Tab on the 3D map and a "Tekstversie" button pops up, with a bright ring

Easier now if you:
👀 don't see so well
⌨️ use a keyboard
🔊 use a screen reader
🌀 get dizzy from motion

🔗 Link in the comments 👇

## 2/4

👀 Easier to read and tap

📸 The line badges stand out more: white on the blue 52, black on the yellow 54. And the new "Station kiezen" list opens any station, no map needed

• Faint grey text is darker
• No more tiny letters
• Buttons big enough for shaky hands 🙌

## 3/4

⌨️ No mouse? No problem!

📸 Tab to "52 Noord · 1 min" in De Pijp's departure card, press Enter, and the map follows that train: "Volgen → Noord" plus its full train card

🔍 Zoom with + and −, no dragging needed

A bright ring always shows where you are ✨

## 4/4

🔊 Screen readers now read GVB's messages out loud, like "line 52: a train is cancelled"

🌀 Asked your phone or computer to "reduce motion"? The map stops swooping and pulsing

🇳🇱🇬🇧 All in Dutch or English

Checked against the WCAG 2.2 guidelines ✅
